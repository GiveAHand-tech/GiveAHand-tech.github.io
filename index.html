Our Mission

GIVE-A-HAND.tech’s mission is to serve as an adoptive home for people with, or who could benefit from, abandoned implantable medical devices. GIVE-A-HAND.tech will enable people to gain access to, and keep benefitting from, these devices for the remainder of their lifetimes.

Our Services

We offer a range of services to enable people with implanted devices to keep using them even after the devices have been abandoned by the commercial market. Check back soon for a "Request for Proposals" that will support researchers who want to continue to provide support to their users.

Our Team

We have decades of experience helping maintain implanted devices for people who benefit from them. If you'd like to be part of this team, let us know!
