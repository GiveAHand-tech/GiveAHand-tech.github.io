Our Mission

Our mission is to enable people with disabilities to have full and continued access to advanced technology that can improve their independence and quality of life. Our focus is on maintaining access to beneficial active implantable devices that have been abandoned by the commercial market.

Our Team

We have decades of experience helping maintain implanted devices for people who benefit from them. If you'd like to be part of this team, let us know!

Our Services

We're just getting off the ground, but we intend to offer a range of services to enable people with implanted devices to keep using them even after the devices have been abandoned by the commercial market.
